Privacy controls

Private mode pauses new history saves and stops history-based recommendations. The personalized feed is off by default: when you switch it on, search phrases made from videos you watched are sent through the relay to a public provider. Neither option makes you anonymous: Cloudflare receives your connection, and the YouTube player connects directly from your device.

Privacy and storage

Your watch history and interests are stored in this browser only and are not encrypted by the app. Search and video metadata normally use a same-origin Cloudflare relay. If the relay cannot provide storyboard data for timeline previews, the app may contact the official public Invidious servers directly from this browser; those servers can see your IP address. Storyboard images may load directly from YouTube image hosts. The embedded YouTube player also connects directly to YouTube. There is no account system or shared server-side history in this version.
